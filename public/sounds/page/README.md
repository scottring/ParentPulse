# Page-turn sounds

Four short paper page flips from "10 book page flips" by starninjas,
https://opengameart.org/content/10-book-page-flips — licensed CC0 (public
domain). Credit is not required; kept here as a courtesy.

Clips 8, 4, 6 and 1 of the pack, trimmed of leading silence, loudness-normalized,
faded out and saved as mono 64 kbps MP3 (`flip-1.mp3` … `flip-4.mp3`). The
reading book plays one per page turn (`src/components/relish/PageSound.tsx`).
