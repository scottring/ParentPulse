# Road trip: "Spot it!" and "Day's highlights"

Scott, 2026-11-18: "can we add a few road trip family games as well, and/or
activities". He picked these two from a list of four (Would you rather and Road
story are parked).

## The situation they're designed for

- One phone, held by a passenger who reads aloud and taps. Nobody else needs a
  screen: the driver can't play, and reading in a moving car makes kids sick.
- Everyone answers out loud. The holder records what was said; nothing is
  private between players (unlike Same page?), so no pass-the-device hand-offs.
- Short, glanceable steps; big tap targets; it must survive the phone locking or
  the app reloading mid-drive (state saved in the book after every tap, like
  games today).
- Clear beginning, middle and end, and something worth keeping at the end.

## 1. Spot it! (game)

- **Start:** pick who's in the car (reuse the game sheet's player picker). A
  card of 9 things to spot, age-fitted to the youngest player (under-6: big,
  common things: a cow, a red car, a bridge, a dog in a car…; older: a license
  plate from another state, a water tower, a horse trailer, a sign with a
  number over 100…). Option to "Swap this one" for a single square. One line:
  "Call it out when you see one. Whoever spots it first gets the square."
- **Middle:** the card fills the screen; tap a square → tap who spotted it (or
  "We all did"). Squares show the spotter's initial. A row or full card is
  celebrated lightly, never required. "Pause here" keeps the card for later in
  the drive.
- **End:** "Finish round" any time. Reveal: "Your road-trip card", the filled
  squares with who spotted what, then a secondary line: "Anna spotted the most:
  4." Same ending pattern as the other games (one column; Finish round; quiet
  Add a memory and Create a shared page).
- **Kept:** the card is remembered with the round in the hosting book (like
  other games' answers), with an optional "Where were you headed?" line so Your
  book shows "Spot it! on the way to Grandma's".

## 2. Day's highlights (activity)

- **Start:** "End of a driving day? Everyone gets three quick questions, out
  loud." Pick who's in the car.
- **Middle:** for each person in turn: "Best part of today?", "Hardest part?",
  "Looking forward to?". The holder types or dictates (reuse the existing voice
  writer), or taps a few age-fitted quick picks for kids (e.g. "the snacks",
  "the swimming pool", "being stuck in the car"). Anyone can skip any question.
- **End:** read-back of the whole car's day, grouped by person, then "Keep as a
  trip page" (one tap; kept in the hosting book, dated, with an optional place
  name). Doing it again on later days builds a trip: Your book shows "Day 1,
  Day 2…" for pages with the same trip name.
